# Xyool OSE — Forward Evidence State (FES)

Status: **PROVISIONAL OSE LEADING-EVIDENCE / INFORMATION-GAP STATE — NO INDEPENDENT CAPITAL AUTHORITY**

This file extends `opportunity-state-engine.md` with a Forward Evidence State designed to identify lawful, public, causally relevant evidence that an economic event is forming **before** that event is fully visible in issuer financial statements, press releases, or market consensus.

FES is the formal version of the Xyool **information-gap radar**.

## Purpose

Forward Evidence State answers:

> **How far has publicly observable upstream evidence progressed toward a company-specific economic event that is not yet fully realized or financially recognized?**

It is designed for situations where the causal chain leaves public breadcrumbs before the issuer reports the economic result.

Examples include:
- government requirements, RFIs, solicitations, procurement notices, contract modifications, subawards, or award ceilings;
- qualification, Approved-for-Use, certification, field testing, evaluation, training, and integration activity;
- prime-contractor or customer disclosures that identify a supplier, architecture, component, or platform relationship;
- initial paid orders before broad rollout;
- production authorizations, funded follow-ons, purchase orders, delivery schedules, or fielding activity before revenue recognition;
- regulatory, permitting, capacity, deployment, or supply-chain evidence that precedes financial conversion.

The intended causal path is:

```text
REQUIREMENT / NEED
        ↓
QUALIFICATION / INTEGRATION
        ↓
FUNDED OR AUTHORIZED PROCUREMENT PATH
        ↓
IDENTIFIABLE ORDER / QUANTITY / FOLLOW-ON
        ↓
FIELDING / DELIVERY / ECONOMIC CONVERSION IMMINENT
        ↓
CASCADE / BACKLOG / REVENUE / CASH
```

FES is most useful in the gap between **narrative possibility** and **realized Cascade / Conversion evidence**.

## Authority boundary

FES belongs inside OSE as a **research-priority, state-transition, and early-evidence variable**.

It may:
- increase research priority;
- identify where to monitor before an issuer reports the outcome;
- strengthen the interpretation of Structural Confirmation when evidence is independent and causally linked;
- identify a likely transition into Cascade Score, backlog, Runway Lock-In, or Conversion Confirmation;
- improve catalyst timing analysis and PR / nonlinear-repricing proximity;
- inform CAC only through the complete OSE state.

It may not:
- create a position by itself;
- set or mechanically increase a CAC weight;
- promote Conversion Confirmation without actual company-specific economic conversion;
- promote Cascade Score without real deployment / repeat-adoption evidence;
- treat a budget authorization, contract ceiling, demo, integration, MOU, or qualification as a funded production order;
- override Friction, shareholder-capture failure, dilution, financing risk, governance failure, or F5 conditions;
- use price action as proof that a fundamental event is forming;
- rely on material non-public information, unauthorized access, hacking, deception, or private-data acquisition.

FES is a **public-evidence inference layer**, not an MNPI-seeking process.

## FES ladder — FES0 to FES5

### FES0 — No forward evidence
No traceable upstream evidence exists beyond narrative, TAM, management aspiration, or general sector demand.

### FES1 — Requirement forming
A documented need, budget line, RFI, sources-sought notice, solicitation, program requirement, qualification process, or customer problem is visible, but no company-specific funded or integrated path is established.

Typical evidence:
- official requirement;
- RFI / RFP / sources-sought;
- funded sector program with no supplier linkage yet;
- early qualification invitation;
- public customer need matching the product.

### FES2 — Qualification / integration evidence
The company/product is linked to a named customer, prime, vehicle, program, architecture, site, or qualification path, but broad commercial production or funded scale is not yet established.

Typical evidence:
- named testing / evaluation;
- system integration;
- Approved-for-Use / certification milestone;
- customer or prime identifies the supplier/product;
- demonstration inside the target architecture;
- initial non-scale trial or qualification deployment.

### FES3 — Funded or authorized procurement path
There is a credible, company-specific path from qualification/integration to paid procurement, but scale conversion is not yet proven.

Typical evidence:
- initial paid order;
- funded program plus supplier-specific qualification;
- contract vehicle or subcontract path capable of placing orders;
- approved production / procurement status;
- funded follow-on authorization;
- named customer indicates intent to procure subject to evaluation or final acceptance.

FES3 means **the path to money is real**. It does not mean the money is material yet.

### FES4 — Identifiable order / quantity / follow-on evidence
A specific economic event is visible before full financial recognition.

Typical evidence:
- purchase order;
- funded task / delivery order;
- subcontract with disclosed dollars or quantities;
- production release;
- repeat order;
- explicit funded unit/site count;
- customer/prime disclosure identifying a material supplier-linked order.

FES4 should normally have evidence that is independently traceable to a real economic commitment.

### FES5 — Economic conversion imminent
Public evidence indicates that the event is moving into delivery, fielding, backlog realization, invoicing, or revenue recognition on a near-term basis.

Typical evidence:
- delivery schedule underway;
- fielding / installation / training tied to funded units;
- disclosed shipment quantities;
- acceptance milestone completed;
- backlog conversion window identified;
- production is underway against a funded order;
- customer deploys the ordered system before the issuer has fully reported the financial effect.

FES5 is not CC. It means the public evidence suggests CC / CS / RL evidence should appear soon if execution proceeds.

## FES velocity

Track level and velocity separately:

- accelerating ↑↑
- improving ↑
- stable →
- weakening ↓
- collapsing ↓↓

A rising FES with no movement for an extended period should decay in confidence even if the nominal rung does not change.

## Forward evidence quality — FE1 to FE4

Every FES state must record source quality separately from the level.

### FE1 — Indirect / exploratory
A plausible public clue exists, but identity, economics, timing, or causal linkage is materially inferred.

Examples:
- product-feature fingerprinting;
- conference/demo presence;
- unofficial trade reporting;
- management description without external corroboration.

### FE2 — Named counterparty / program
The customer, prime, platform, program, site, or architecture is explicit, but order economics, quantity, or realization remain incomplete.

### FE3 — Direct official economic evidence
Primary-source evidence shows a contract, purchase, funded task, order, quantity, delivery, official procurement status, regulatory approval, or equivalent company-specific economic step.

### FE4 — Independently corroborated direct evidence
At least two genuinely independent evidence streams confirm the same event, with at least one direct primary or official source.

Do not count syndicated copies, reposts, mirrored releases, or repeated management statements as independent corroboration.

## Required inference discipline

FES permits inference, but inference must be explicit.

For any unnamed customer/program/platform mapping, record:

```text
Identity Inference: Candidate / Probable / Confirmed
Identity Confidence: Low / Medium / High
Matching Features:
Contradictory Evidence:
What Would Confirm:
```

Rules:
- Candidate means plausible but weakly identified.
- Probable means multiple distinctive features align and no strong contradiction is known.
- Confirmed requires explicit primary-source linkage.

A probable identity may increase research priority, but cannot by itself promote FES to FE3/FE4, CS, CC, or RL.

## Public breadcrumb map

For each company, map the upstream public information chain most likely to lead the issuer's own reporting.

Potential sources include:

```text
BUDGET / REQUIREMENT
    ↓
SOLICITATION / PROCUREMENT FORECAST
    ↓
PRIME CONTRACT / PROGRAM VEHICLE
    ↓
SUBCONTRACT / SUPPLIER LINK
    ↓
QUALIFICATION / INTEGRATION
    ↓
PURCHASE ORDER / DELIVERY ORDER
    ↓
FIELDING / TRAINING / DEPLOYMENT
    ↓
BACKLOG / REVENUE / CASH
```

The exact chain is industry-specific.

Examples:
- defense: agency → prime → subcontract → unit fielding;
- semiconductors: platform qualification → design win → wafer/order evidence → production ramp;
- data centers: permit/power/interconnect → equipment order → construction → energization;
- biotech: trial milestone → regulator → label / reimbursement → launch orders;
- industrials: customer capex → equipment award → installation → utilization;
- mining/materials: qualification → offtake → financing → construction → first production.

## Relationship to Cascade Score

FES and Cascade Score measure different parts of the same causal path.

- **FES asks:** is a future company-specific economic event forming before full realization?
- **CS asks:** has a real deployment begun replicating across units, sites, customers, programs, geographies, or architectures?

Typical transition:

```text
FES1-2
requirement / qualification
        ↓
FES3
funded or authorized path
        ↓
FES4
specific order / quantity
        ↓
FES5
fielding / conversion imminent
        ↓
CS2 / CS3 and/or CC update
```

FES does not mechanically promote CS.

A company can be FES4 with only CS1 if a large production order exists before deployment. Conversely, a company can have high CS with low current FES if the existing rollout is already mature and no new upstream event is forming.

## Relationship to Structural Confirmation

Independent qualification, customer selection, prime integration, regulatory approval, sole/limited-source procurement, or repeated official customer reliance can strengthen SC.

However:
- a demo does not prove a bottleneck;
- a named integration does not prove switching costs;
- a funded program does not prove the company will capture it;
- a requirement does not prove durable control.

FES may contribute evidence to SC, but SC must still be scored on control, necessity, replacement difficulty, qualification barriers, and independent validation.

## Relationship to Conversion Confirmation

FES never promotes CC mechanically.

CC remains based on actual conversion:

```text
ORDERS → BOOKINGS → BACKLOG → DEPLOYMENT → REVENUE → MARGIN → EBITDA → OCF → FCF → PER-SHARE VALUE
```

When an FES event becomes visible in bookings/backlog/revenue/cash, update CC from the realized economics and close or advance the FES event separately.

## Relationship to Runway Lock-In

FES can inform RL only when the evidence is legally or economically binding enough to protect future value.

Examples that may support RL:
- funded backlog;
- take-or-pay;
- binding offtake;
- minimum-volume commitments;
- funded multi-year delivery orders;
- sole-source / qualification positions tied to committed programs.

Do not increase RL for:
- TAM;
- budget ceilings;
- unfunded options;
- demos;
- non-binding MOUs;
- vague pipeline;
- management expectations.

## Relationship to Recognition, RR, and PR

FES is most valuable when upstream evidence is improving faster than market recognition.

A particularly useful configuration is:

```text
FES2 → FES3 / FES4 with ↑ or ↑↑ velocity
+ FE2/FE3/FE4 evidence
+ high remaining Potential
+ credible SC
+ manageable Friction
+ incomplete Recognition
+ CS / CC not yet fully advanced
```

This can increase confidence that a PR / nonlinear-repricing event is becoming more proximate.

But:
- FES does not create RR by itself;
- FES does not prove a rerating will occur;
- price appreciation is not FES evidence;
- a high FES event can fail, be delayed, be too small, or already be priced.

## Relationship to Friction and shareholder capture

Every high FES event must be tested against the fully diluted capitalization and financing path.

Ask:
- Will the event require an equity raise before it converts?
- Is there an ATM, shelf, ELOC, convert, preferred, warrant, or resale overhang?
- Does working capital consume the event's economics?
- Does the company have enough cash to fulfill the order?
- Does a prime/customer capture most of the economics?
- Is margin likely to expand or compress at scale?

High FES + destructive financing can still be a poor equity outcome.

## Anti-false-positive rules

Do not promote FES merely because:
- a government budget mentions the sector;
- a company attends a conference;
- a customer tests a product once;
- a contract has a large maximum ceiling;
- an IDIQ exists without funded task orders;
- a shelf registration exists;
- an Approved-for-Use designation exists without procurement evidence;
- a partner integrates a product without customer acceptance;
- a product appears compatible with a platform;
- management calls the pipeline large;
- social media speculates about a customer;
- the stock price rises.

Mandatory distinctions:

```text
AUTHORIZATION ≠ OBLIGATION
CONTRACT CEILING ≠ FUNDED ORDER
QUALIFICATION ≠ PROCUREMENT
INTEGRATION ≠ PRODUCTION ADOPTION
DEMO ≠ CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE
REGISTERED SHARES ≠ ISSUED SHARES
INITIAL ORDER ≠ CASCADE
BACKLOG ≠ REVENUE
REVENUE ≠ CASH
```

## Negative-evidence rule

FES must record failed searches and contradictory evidence when material.

Examples:
- incumbent supplier remains specified in a new procurement;
- expected follow-on order does not appear;
- customer cancels or delays the program;
- qualification expires or fails;
- budget is cut;
- prime removes supplier from architecture;
- order is materially smaller than expected;
- production begins with a competitor's component.

Negative evidence can reduce FES confidence, velocity, or level.

Do not hide negative evidence merely because the broader thesis remains attractive.

## Decay rule

A forward signal loses value if it does not convert within the expected window.

For every FES2+ event record:

```text
Expected Conversion Window:
Last Confirming Evidence Date:
Age of Signal:
Decay Status: Fresh / Aging / Stale
```

Suggested research convention, not a capital rule:
- Fresh: still inside the expected conversion window;
- Aging: beyond the midpoint of the expected window with no new evidence;
- Stale: beyond the expected window without conversion or renewed confirmation.

Stale FES should normally lose confidence even before a formal rung downgrade.


This creates a testable dataset rather than allowing hindsight-based storytelling.

## Validation metrics

FES is not considered production-valid merely because individual examples look compelling.

Point-in-time validation should measure:
- median lead time versus issuer financial disclosure;
- FES3→realized hit rate;
- FES4→realized hit rate;
- false-positive rate by evidence quality;
- time-decay calibration;
- incremental information versus OSE without FES;
- incremental information versus Cascade Score alone;
- whether FE3/FE4 materially outperform FE1/FE2;
- whether identity inference produces unacceptable false positives;
- whether adding FES improves discovery and CAC judgment without inducing premature entries or excessive chasing.

Backtests must preserve original timestamps and prevent look-ahead.

## Initial CODA calibration — September 2026

The CODA test that motivated this module produced a useful separation between **real evidence**, **probabilistic identity inference**, and **missing proof**.

### Untethered DAVD — U.S. Navy

Approximate provisional state:

```text
FES3 ↑
FE3
Identity: Confirmed
```

Why:
- Approved Navy Use / qualification path is complete;
- Navy demand and procurement path are real;
- prior DAVD development / unit procurement paths are traceable;
- management expects procurement after approval;
- no new post-approval material quantity order was independently found in the initial test.

Therefore the path to procurement is real, but the missing event is still a material post-approval order or funded unit count.

Promotion trigger:
- funded post-approval order / task / direct purchase with identifiable quantity or economics → FES4.

### NANO — unnamed established vehicle program

Approximate provisional state:

```text
FES3 ↑
FE2-FE3
Identity: Confirmed only as an unnamed established vehicle program
```

Why:
- CODA disclosed an initial paid NANO order for evaluation in an established vehicle program;
- follow-on scale depends on successful evaluation;
- no material production quantity was disclosed.

Promotion trigger:
- repeat / production order, explicit quantity, or funded follow-on → FES4 and likely CS2→CS3 review.

### Hammerhead candidate mapping

Approximate provisional state:

```text
FES2-FES3 ↑
FE1-FE2
Identity Inference: Probable, not Confirmed
```

Why:
- public Hammerhead integration uses NANO and DAVD Flex for real-time 3D perception / navigation / obstacle avoidance;
- this closely matches the functional description of the unnamed established vehicle program;
- live SOF demonstration increases relevance;
- the public evidence does not yet explicitly prove that Hammerhead is the unnamed paid-order vehicle program.

This is exactly why identity inference must be separated from economic confirmation.

Promotion trigger:
- explicit CODA / Patriot3 / Shark Marine / customer statement linking the paid program to Hammerhead, or a funded Hammerhead order containing NANO → FE3/FES4 depending economics.

### VideoRay Defender / MESR path

Approximate provisional state:

```text
FES2 →
FE2-FE3
Identity: Confirmed historical integration/evaluation path
```

Positive evidence:
- long Navy evaluation history;
- funded Defender/MESR program scale;
- CODA compatibility/integration evidence.

Negative evidence:
- at least one newer Navy Defender procurement specified an incumbent competing sonar rather than CODA.

Therefore this path remains strategically relevant but is not promoted to FES3/FES4 without fresh company-specific procurement evidence.

## Initial validation status

**PROVISIONAL CONCEPT PASS from the CODA information-gap test.**

The test showed that FES can add information not captured cleanly by trailing CC or realized CS:
- it exposed a real upstream procurement chain;
- it separated confirmed program evidence from inferred platform identity;
- it surfaced a post-quarter vehicle integration / demonstration signal;
- it also surfaced negative evidence and the absence of a hidden post-approval DAVD quantity order.

This is sufficient to formalize FES as an OSE research/state extension.

It is **not sufficient** to authorize any independent capital rule.

Future cross-stock testing should include multiple industries and both successful and failed forward signals before any stronger authority is considered.
