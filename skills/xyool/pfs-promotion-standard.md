# Xyool — PFS Promotion + Hazard Standard

Status: **CANONICAL PROCESS FOR PFS / HAZARD PROMOTION — NUMERIC ENTRY RUNGS REMAIN PROVISIONAL**

This file tightens `pre-funded-event-hunter.md` after the first state × hazard replay.

The purpose is to stop three errors:
1. promoting a procurement state because the eventual order happened quickly in hindsight;
2. confusing procurement maturity with time-to-order;
3. increasing tactical exposure because time or price passed without new causal evidence.

The governing rule is:

> **State promotion requires new causal evidence. HOT promotion requires a current timing anchor. Price and hindsight cannot supply either.**

---

## 1. Identity lock before promotion

Every PFS3+ event must preserve the exact target as far as public evidence allows:

```text
Company
Customer / Agency / Program
Product / Project / Scope
Target Funded Event
Current Causal Gate
Evidence Date
```

If the target customer/program is only inferred, preserve the inference state and confidence. An unnamed aggregate pipeline cannot be silently promoted to a specific PFS4/PFS5 event.

---

## 2. PFS state promotion

PFS answers:

> **How mature is the causal path to the target funded event?**

It does not answer how many days remain.

### PFS0 → PFS1

Require a real test/prototype/evaluation path beyond narrative interest.

### PFS1 → PFS2

Require customer-funded development, paid pilot, initial limited order, low-rate activity, or another real economic commitment while material technical/commercial gates remain.

### PFS2 → PFS3 — accepted / production-validated

Require evidence that at least one meaningful validation gate has actually been crossed, for example:
- qualification or acceptance completed;
- customer is using the product in production;
- first meaningful commercial deployment is active;
- first production order or equivalent paid deployment realized;
- repeat-customer relationship is demonstrated.

Do not promote from prototype success alone when customer acceptance or production use remains unproven.

### PFS3 → PFS4 — procurement path visible

Require a **specific mechanism capable of producing the target funded event**, not merely evidence that the customer likes the product.

Qualifying examples:
- customer/agency funding or budget authorization tied to the program;
- usable contract / IDIQ / framework / master agreement path;
- long-lead material or capacity authorization for anticipated production;
- customer-specific product selection followed by a known procurement path;
- repeat-order mechanism with a genuinely comparable funded prior unit;
- customer request for production capacity tied to the target program;
- existing-customer adjacent-scope process that has advanced beyond generic cross-sell discussion.

A strong PFS3 state without a visible procurement mechanism remains PFS3.

### PFS4 → PFS5 — direct order-specific evidence

PFS5 requires evidence aimed at **the funded order/award itself**, not merely at an upstream enabler.

Required:
1. target event identity is locked strongly enough to audit;
2. procurement/funding is the next material economic gate or no more than one narrow administrative/acceptance gate remains;
3. fresh evidence refers directly to the expected order, award, task order, production release, or equivalent funded commitment.

Qualifying examples:
- management explicitly expects the specific follow-on production order;
- customer/agency says a specific award/order is being processed or is the next step;
- final acceptance is complete and a documented purchase commitment remains subject only to procurement completion;
- a customer-requested production-capacity step is explicitly tied to the pending funded award/order;
- an established repeat program has a current, customer-specific order signal beyond cadence alone.

Not sufficient by itself:
- long-lead materials authorization;
- generic follow-on discussion;
- an IDIQ ceiling;
- broad backlog/pipeline language;
- one historical repeat interval;
- capacity construction without a target customer order path.

Those may support PFS4 but not PFS5.

### PFS5 → PFS6

The funded event is realized. Close the pre-funded timer immediately and decide whether the event is:
- a harvest point;
- a runner / remaining-recognition state;
- or the start of a downstream deployment/revenue/repeat timer.

## 3. Hazard Heat promotion

Hazard Heat answers:

> **How near does the target funded event appear to be now?**

### COLD

Use when the target is still upstream, multiple material gates remain, or no supported near window exists.

### WARM

Use when the causal procurement path is credible but timing is broad or insufficiently anchored.

A high PFS state can remain WARM for months.

### HOT — hard gates

HOT is intentionally difficult to earn.

All of the following must be true:

```text
TARGET IDENTITY LOCKED
AND PFS >= 3
AND CURRENT PROGRAM EVIDENCE IS FRESH
AND NO MORE THAN ONE MATERIAL PRE-FUNDING GATE REMAINS
AND AT LEAST ONE QUALIFIED TIMING ANCHOR EXISTS
```

Qualified timing anchors:

1. **Direct near-term language** tied to the exact target event: `near term`, `shortly`, `this quarter`, `by year-end`, or another bounded phrase from a primary/issuer source.
2. **Binding or operational deadline**: budget expiration, contractual deadline, required delivery/start date, procurement calendar, or similar constraint that forces a short window.
3. **Established repeat cadence entering its supported window**: normally at least two comparable intervals, same event type/mechanism, plus fresh evidence that the exact customer/program remains active.
4. **Customer-required near-term capacity / production need**: a dated or bounded customer requirement where the funded order is necessary to satisfy the disclosed production/delivery plan.

One timing anchor can be sufficient only when the causal linkage is direct and the other HOT hard gates pass. Weak/inferred timing should remain WARM.

---

## 4. What does NOT make an event HOT

Do not promote WARM → HOT because:
- the stock price rises;
- volume rises;
- the expected order eventually arrives quickly;
- calendar time passes;
- management repeats an old undated statement;
- a generic follow-on is discussed with no near-time descriptor;
- one first→second repeat interval exists;
- final acceptance occurs without any timing evidence for procurement;
- an upstream material/capacity authorization exists without a near order window;
- an analog stock converted quickly.

**One observed cadence interval remains Emerging under RPT and cannot by itself create HOT.**

---

## 5. Promotion must be evidence-driven

A higher tactical rung requires one of:
- PFS state promotion;
- Hazard Heat promotion;
- an independent causal confirmation that materially reduces a different uncertainty.

The following are not add triggers:
- time passage alone;
- price strength alone;
- unchanged management language;
- duplicate reporting of the same fact.

Use the counterfactual test:

> **If this new item were removed, would the causal state, timing hazard, or remaining uncertainty actually change?**

If no, do not climb the ladder.

---

## 6. Demotion / decay

### HOT → WARM

Demote when:
- the bounded near-term window passes without realization;
- the timing anchor expires or is withdrawn;
- fresh program evidence becomes stale;
- an additional material gate appears;
- customer timing slips without a credible replacement window.

Do not silently roll HOT forward.

### WARM decay

Use the current research schedule for PFS4/WARM-type tactical exposure:

```text
~60 calendar days without causal progression
→ no add; normally halve the tactical starter

~120 calendar days without progression / explicit reaffirmation
→ normally close/reset the remaining tactical sleeve
```

These durations remain provisional calibration points.

### State demotion

Demote PFS if the underlying causal path itself weakens: failed test/acceptance, lost customer, funding removal, architecture change, competitor displacement, or evidence that the procurement mechanism is no longer usable.

---

## 7. Recognition is an execution cap, not a causal promotion

PFS and Hazard Heat describe the economic process. Recognition describes how much of the expected price move may already have occurred.

Therefore:

```text
RAW PFS/HEAT RUNG
→ cap by remaining Profit View / Recognition
→ cap by TTC desired exposure
→ cap by Quant / liquidity / portfolio risk
→ executable tactical exposure
```

If recognition is HIGH/EXHAUSTED, an event may remain PFS5/HOT while the correct action is **do not add / harvest**.

Do not falsify the causal state merely to express a lower trading allocation.

## 8. Cross-module boundaries

These states are deliberately not interchangeable:

- **Breadcrumb Detection** — detects new causal information and decides WATCHING / ARMED / ACTIVE.
- **Causal Timer Framework** — defines what transition clock is running.
- **FES** — broad forward-evidence state across requirement → qualification → funded path → order → fielding.
- **PFS** — narrow maturity of the path to a specific not-yet-realized funded event.
- **Hazard Heat** — proximity of that funded event.
- **Cascade Score** — actual replication after meaningful deployment/authorization evidence.
- **Repeat-Cadence / RPT** — timing structure of genuinely comparable repeated events.
- **TTC** — capital priority among qualified opportunities.
- **Event Execution** — translates TTC-approved exposure into entry/harvest/re-buy mechanics.

### Important numbering guardrail

`FES4` and `PFS4` do **not** mean the same thing merely because both use the number 4.

Never map levels numerically between FES, PFS, CS, SC, CC, RL, or other ladders.

## 9. Tightened training replay

The v1 calibration initially labeled VUZI `PFS4/HOT` because the production-order realization followed 11 days after the frozen production-ready breadcrumb.

That is too permissive under strict anti-lookahead because the frozen record does not preserve a qualified near-term timing anchor.

Revised frozen interpretation:

```text
AEHR   PFS5 / HOT
VUZI   PFS4 / WARM
KOPN   PFS4 / WARM
OESX   PFS3-PFS4 / WARM
POCI   PFS4 / WARM
IPX    PFS2 / COLD
AMPX   PFS2-PFS3 / COLD
```

The VUZI demotion is a process correction, not a claim that its breadcrumb was weak. It was a strong procurement-state signal whose near timing was not earned from the preserved pre-event evidence.

Using the clean AEHR/VUZI/KOPN price replay and the stricter entry rungs:

```text
AEHR PFS5/HOT  -> 75% sleeve
VUZI PFS4/WARM -> 25% sleeve
KOPN PFS4/WARM -> 25% sleeve

median desired-sleeve contribution through event ≈ +20.86%
worst weighted close-based adverse move           ≈ -12.37%
mean pre-event full-equivalent capital-days       ≈ 15.42
mean with current WARM decay approximation        ≈ 10.42
```

The previous looser ladder also had a ~+20.86% sample median and ~-12.37% worst weighted adverse move, but used ~16.33 mean full-equivalent capital-days before decay. Tightening HOT therefore reduced capital use in this tiny training sample without reducing the median desired-sleeve contribution.

This is in-sample process calibration, not out-of-sample proof.

## 10. Validation boundary

Canonical now:
- separate PFS state from Hazard Heat;
- require new causal evidence for state promotion;
- require a qualified timing anchor for HOT;
- one cadence interval cannot independently create HOT;
- price/hindsight cannot promote PFS or Heat;
- recognition caps execution rather than rewriting causal state;
- stale HOT states must demote rather than roll forward silently.

Still provisional:
- exact tactical percentages by PFS/Heat cell;
- 60/120-day WARM decay constants;
- any universal 30/60/90-day order probability;
- recognition-consumed numeric thresholds;
- automatic classifier weights.

Prospective calibration must include every detected PFS3-PFS5 candidate, including stalls and failures.
