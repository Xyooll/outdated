# Xyool — Pre-Funded Event Hunter

Status: **PROVISIONAL RESEARCH / TTC INPUT — STATE + HAZARD MODEL UNDER CALIBRATION**

## Purpose

Find economically material funded orders, contracts, task orders, production awards, scope expansions, and similar shareholder-relevant funding events **before the final headline**.

The target is not the announcement day. The target is the public causal state immediately before funding becomes likely enough and near enough to justify tactical exposure.

```text
PUBLIC BREADCRUMB
→ COMMERCIAL / PROCUREMENT STATE
→ FUNDED-EVENT HAZARD
→ TTC ENTRY AUTHORITY
→ FUNDED ORDER / CONTRACT
→ PROFIT HARVEST / NEXT TIMER
```

This module does not override Quality, shareholder capture, Recognition, Quant caps, liquidity, or event-integrity requirements.

---

## 1. Pre-Funded State (PFS)

Keep state and timing hazard separate.

### PFS0 — NARRATIVE

Interest, TAM, generic pipeline, non-specific demand commentary.

Capital authority: none.

### PFS1 — TEST / PROTOTYPE

Prototype, unpaid or lightly funded test, technical evaluation, R&D exploration, early sample activity.

Capital authority: research only unless another independent thesis supports ownership.

### PFS2 — PAID PILOT / DEVELOPMENT

Customer-funded development, paid pilot, early-access program, initial small order, limited-rate activity, or qualification work with real customer economics but multiple gates remaining.

Capital authority: normally no dedicated pre-funded-event sleeve.

### PFS3 — ACCEPTED / PRODUCTION-VALIDATED

Evidence that the product/system is accepted, qualified, deployed, in production use, or has completed a meaningful first paid deployment. The next economic step can plausibly be repeat procurement or scale-up.

Examples:
- existing production customer;
- successful qualification / acceptance;
- first meaningful commercial deployment;
- first production order realized;
- repeat customer relationship proven.

Capital authority: small starter only when Quality, materiality and recognition are favorable.

### PFS4 — PROCUREMENT PATH VISIBLE

A specific repeat/funding mechanism is visible.

Examples:
- long-lead materials authorization for anticipated production;
- framework / IDIQ / master agreement with an actionable order path;
- same-customer repeat cadence;
- customer budget / funding authorization;
- production capacity request tied to customer demand;
- pilot explicitly scaling toward production;
- existing-customer scope expansion after testing / product selection;
- commercial/legal vehicle already in place.

Capital authority: tactical starter can be justified, but timing can still be wide.

### PFS5 — DIRECT HIGH-SPECIFICITY ORDER EVIDENCE

Direct evidence that the funded event is the next near economic gate.

Examples:
- management explicitly expects a significant near-term follow-on production order;
- final acceptance gate has passed and procurement is the remaining gate;
- customer has requested near-term capacity tied to a production award;
- production-ready state plus specific imminent commercial conversion language;
- repeat cadence is due and independently supported by current customer/program evidence.

Capital authority: strongest pre-headline state, still subject to hazard, recognition and risk caps.

### PFS6 — FUNDED EVENT REALIZED

The order/contract/funding event has happened.

Close the pre-funded timer. Do not keep awarding imminent-event credit. Decide whether the event is a harvest point, a runner state, or the start of a new downstream timer.

## 2. Hazard Heat

PFS answers **how mature is the causal procurement state?**

Hazard Heat answers **how near does the funded event appear to be?**

### COLD

No supported near window. Multiple gates or unclear customer timing remain.

### WARM

A credible funding/procurement mechanism exists, but timing is broad. Same-company/process analogs imply months rather than days/weeks, or no direct near-term language exists.

### HOT

The final commercial gate is close and at least one strong timing signal exists:
- explicit `near-term` / `shortly` / equivalent management language tied to the exact order;
- customer is already in production and follow-on procurement is explicitly expected;
- supported same-customer cadence is entering its empirically observed window;
- final acceptance / product-selection gate has passed;
- a contractual or budget deadline makes funding unusually near.

Do not label an event HOT from stock momentum alone.

## 3. Evidence hierarchy

For every candidate prefer:
1. customer / government / regulator / procurement source;
2. company filing;
3. company earnings call or issuer release;
4. explicit same-program history;
5. same-company / same-customer cadence;
6. close process analogs;
7. broad industry analogs last.

A valid candidate must preserve:

```text

Customer / Program
Product / Project
Current PFS state
Evidence date

Funding mechanism
Materiality vs equity / revenue

```

## 4. Current provisional entry ladder

Express exposure as a percentage of **TTC-approved catalyst sleeve**, not total portfolio.

The current training calibration supports a state × hazard ladder rather than a universal one-third schedule.

| State / Heat | Provisional tactical exposure |
|---|---:|
| PFS0-PFS2 | **0%** |
| PFS3 / WARM | **0-15%** |
| PFS3 / HOT | **10-20%** |
| PFS4 / COLD | **0-10%** |
| PFS4 / WARM | **20-30%** |
| PFS4 / HOT | **40-50%** |
| PFS5 / COLD | **20-30%** |
| PFS5 / WARM | **40-60%** |
| PFS5 / HOT | **60-75%** |

Research defaults for replay are 15%, 25%, 50%, and 75% at the relevant rungs.

### Pre-event cap

Do not automatically deploy 100% of the desired catalyst sleeve before an unscheduled funded event.

Reserve at least ~25% for one of:
- independent causal confirmation;
- recognition reset / evidence-gated re-buy;
- post-event continuation when meaningful downstream Profit View remains;
- cash if the event arrives already fully recognized.

This reserve is provisional and subject to broader calibration.

## 5. Decay / capital-efficiency rule

A strong breadcrumb can be correct but too early.

Therefore tactical exposure must decay when time passes without causal progression.

Current research schedule for PFS4/WARM-type events:

```text
~60 calendar days without new causal progression
→ do not add; normally reduce the tactical starter by roughly half

~120 calendar days without progression or explicit reaffirmation
→ normally close the remaining tactical sleeve / reset the timer

Fresh primary evidence that the exact procurement path remains active
→ may re-arm the state and start a new supported window
```

These are calibration points, not permanent constants.

An earned thesis sleeve is separate and does not automatically close when the tactical timer decays.

---

## 6. Recognition control

The goal is not merely to be early; it is to own **remaining** recognition.

At every add ask:

```text
How much of the current Profit View has already been captured since the actionable breadcrumb?
```

If a stock has already moved through a large fraction of its event-class Profit View without new economics:
- do not climb the entry ladder solely because the headline has not yet appeared;
- hold or harvest depending on remaining economics;
- require new causal information before adding.

A funded headline can arrive after the market has already repriced it. KOPN-style material authorization → later follow-on is an explicit calibration example.

## 7. Scanner questions

For each filing, call, presentation, customer release, government award/procurement notice, or company release ask:

1. Is there an existing customer relationship?
2. Is the customer already using the product in production?
3. Has testing / acceptance / qualification completed?
4. Is there a paid pilot or funded development program?
5. Is a procurement vehicle already in place?
6. Has the customer authorized long-lead materials, capacity, or budget?
7. Is a follow-on / production order explicitly discussed?
8. Is management language tied to a concrete time descriptor?
9. Is there same-customer repeat cadence?
10. Is the prospective order economically material to the equity?
11. Is financing/capacity sufficient to fulfill it without destroying capture?
12. Has the stock already repriced most of the likely event economics?
13. What would invalidate or materially delay the order?

Only specific causal evidence can promote PFS. Generic pipeline optimism cannot.

## 8. Historical training findings — v1

The first training set produced a sharp state/hazard distinction:

- **AEHR — PFS5 / HOT:** explicit significant near-term follow-on production order expected from an existing production customer → record $41M order about 8 days after the conservative executable entry.
- **VUZI — PFS4 / HOT:** production-ready deployment state → production order about 11 days later.
- **OESX — PFS3/PFS4 / WARM:** first same-customer facility → second facility about 92 days later; useful repeat-cadence evidence but not an exact pre-order clock.
- **KOPN — PFS4 / WARM:** $5.3M long-lead material authorization for an anticipated future production order → ~$9M follow-on about 150 days later.
- **POCI — PFS4 / WARM:** initial production order + explicit follow-on discussion → material follow-on did not arrive for about 537 days.
- **IPX — PFS2 / COLD:** prototype order with possible larger production path → no scaled Carver production award by the 2026-09-16 freeze.
- **AMPX — upstream PFS2/PFS3 / COLD control:** customer awards improved future-order visibility but did not itself equal a direct funded cell order for the targeted path during the calibration window.

Small sample. These observations calibrate process and hazard shape, not universal probabilities.

### Provisional hazard result

In the two clean **HOT** examples, funded realization occurred in **8 and 11 days**.

In three **WARM** procurement/repeat examples with eventual realization, observed times were approximately **92, 150, and 537 days**; median ~150 days.

Therefore:

> **PFS strength and hazard proximity are not interchangeable. A mature procurement state can still be months early.**

## 9. Validation boundary

Canonical now:
- explicitly search for pre-funded causal states;
- separate PFS from Hazard Heat;
- preserve exact customer/program identity;
- do not let PFS bypass Quality;
- do not let price momentum create PFS/HOT status;
- close/reset the timer when the event realizes;
- decay tactical authority when a WARM signal fails to advance.

Still provisional:
- exact PFS exposure percentages;
- 60/120-day decay constants;
- numeric order-probability priors by state;
- universal recognition-consumed thresholds;
- automatic scanner scoring.

Promotion requires a broad denominator containing both funded realizations and failed/stalled PFS3-PFS5 candidates frozen before outcome.
