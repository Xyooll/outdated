# Xyool — Contract Economic Ladder

Status: **DOMAIN CANONICAL PROCESS; NUMERIC CALIBRATION PROVISIONAL**

## Purpose

Prevent contract, framework, IDIQ, procurement-vehicle, option, and program headlines from being mistaken for committed shareholder economics.

Core rule:

> **Contract ceiling can expand Optioned Economic Surface, but cannot by itself increase Committed Economic Value.**

A large headline may matter strategically while still representing little or no funded revenue.

## Contract Decomposition Rule

Status: **DOMAIN CANONICAL PROCESS**

> **Classify economic obligations, not press releases.**

One announcement may contain multiple economically different components. Do not assign one CE rung or one Commitment Quality state to the entire announcement unless all material components genuinely share the same economics.

Decompose when relevant:

```text
protected minimum | discretionary option
funded base | incentives / options
current purchase order | future framework
contracted sites | future target sites
take-or-pay volume | long-tail service estimate
conditional revenue commitment | actual equipment purchase order
company-specific order | shared vehicle ceiling
```

For each material component independently record:

```text
CE rung
Commitment Quality
Optioned Economic Surface
Committed Economic Value
next CE transition
shareholder-capture friction
```

Then construct the aggregate catalyst interpretation without double-counting.

- 20-case frozen historical validation completed 2026-09-20;
- 12 of 20 tested announcements required material decomposition;
- no prior canonical protection regressed.

## Required separation

For any material contract-like catalyst, preserve these as distinct states:

```text
ACCESS / ELIGIBILITY
OPTIONED ECONOMIC SURFACE
FUNDED / COMMITTED VALUE
BACKLOG / DELIVERY CONVERSION
REVENUE CONVERSION
PROFIT / CASH CONVERSION
SHAREHOLDER CAPTURE
```

Never collapse:

```text
CEILING / MAXIMUM
→ AWARD
→ TASK ORDER / DELIVERY ORDER
→ BACKLOG
→ REVENUE
→ EBITDA / FCF
→ PER-SHARE VALUE
```

into one economic quantity.

## Contract Economic Ladder

### CE0 — Access only
The company is eligible, selected, prequalified, placed on a vehicle, framework, schedule, consortium, or multiple-award pool.

No meaningful economics are yet committed.

### CE1 — Optioned economic surface
The company has contractual or procedural access to plausible future work, but value remains contingent on future orders, exercise of options, customer funding, scope assignment, or competition.

This state can increase strategic potential and future opportunity visibility.

It does **not** by itself create backlog, revenue, earnings, or funded contract value.

### CE2 — Funded / committed order
An identifiable task order, delivery order, purchase order, definitized award, funded increment, minimum guarantee, or other economically committed amount exists.

Record:
- committed amount;
- funding status;
- period of performance;
- cancellation / termination rights;
- whether the amount is incremental;
- whether additional competition remains.

### CE3 — Backlog / delivery conversion
Committed value is entering reported backlog, scheduled deployment, production, shipment, or other company-specific delivery state.

Backlog quality must still be tested for:
- funding;
- cancellation;
- duration;
- margin;
- working capital;
- concentration;
- conversion history.

### CE4 — Revenue conversion
The contract economics are appearing in reported revenue or an equivalent realized operating metric.

Revenue conversion is stronger evidence than the headline award.

### CE5 — Profit / cash / shareholder conversion
Incremental economics reach operating profit, free cash flow, or another durable value measure and remain capturable by common shareholders after financing, dilution, debt, capex, working capital, and valuation already paid.

This is the strongest contract-economic confirmation state.

## Two mandatory quantities

Every material contract catalyst should distinguish:

### Committed Economic Value
Economics presently obligated, funded, ordered, or otherwise contractually committed enough to support a realizable operating path.

### Optioned Economic Surface
Future economics the company may now access but has not yet earned, been assigned, or had funded.

A large Optioned Economic Surface can raise Potential and research priority.

It cannot be treated as if it were Committed Economic Value.

## Contract Commitment Quality

Status: **DOMAIN CANONICAL PROCESS; NUMERIC CALIBRATION PROVISIONAL**

The CE rung describes economic realization. A second, non-numeric state describes how economically protected the apparent commitment is before conversion.

Required state:

```text
PROTECTED
CONDITIONAL
WEAK
UNKNOWN
```

### PROTECTED
Use when the evidence supports a binding, company-specific obligation with meaningful economic protection.

Typical evidence can include:
- explicit binding / undertaking / minimum-purchase language;
- defined quantity or value;
- pricing or pricing mechanics;
- delivery / performance schedule;
- governing law / dispute mechanism;
- no broad buyer termination-for-convenience right disclosed;
- buyer quantity is not freely discretionary;
- conditions mainly concern supplier performance, delivery, homologation, or other execution duties rather than buyer willingness.

PROTECTED does not mean risk-free. Supplier failure, certification, capacity, financing, force majeure, or other contractual conditions may still prevent realization.

### CONDITIONAL
Use when a genuine commitment exists but material conditions can still resize, delay, or release the obligation.

Examples:
- material acceptance / performance conditions;
- quantity reductions tied to supplier non-performance;
- financing / appropriation conditions;
- meaningful conditions precedent;
- termination rights that are narrower than pure convenience but still economically important.

### WEAK
Use when the apparent commitment is largely customer-discretionary or economically easy to exit.

Examples:
- termination for convenience with little disclosed protection;
- no minimum purchase;
- buyer decides actual quantity;
- future purchase orders are the only firm economic commitments;
- non-binding / indicative language;
- framework or master agreement creates access but not meaningful purchase obligation.

### UNKNOWN
Use when public terms are insufficient to classify commitment protection.

For any agreement that appears to establish a minimum purchase or company-specific commitment, inspect:

```text
binding vs indicative language
termination for convenience
termination compensation / remedies
conditions precedent
acceptance / performance criteria
future purchase-order requirement
customer quantity discretion
financing / funding dependencies
supplier production / certification gates
whether already-issued purchase orders survive termination
governing law / enforceability clues
```

Do not assume that the word `minimum`, `definitive`, `order`, or `agreement` automatically creates CE2.

Interaction rule:

```text
CE RUNG = where economics have progressed
COMMITMENT QUALITY = how protected the current commitment is
```

They are separate.

Examples:

```text
CE1 + WEAK
large framework / cancellable master agreement / no firm orders

CE1 + PROTECTED
binding long-horizon minimum with defined buyer obligation,
but individual delivery orders / conversion still remain

CE2 + PROTECTED
specific funded / ordered economics with meaningful contractual protection
```

Commitment Quality does not create a numeric haircut or automatic capital action. It constrains what may be claimed about the reliability of Committed Economic Value.

Promotion evidence:
- GOEV / Walmart 2022: apparent minimum remained broadly cancellable and dependent on future purchase orders -> WEAK / CONDITIONAL, not automatic CE2.
- LCID / Saudi Arabia 2022: binding undertaking, defined 50,000-vehicle commitment, annual quantity bands, pricing mechanics, delivery deadline, governing jurisdiction, and reciprocal supply obligation -> PROTECTED.

## Multiple-award / IDIQ control

For IDIQ, framework, schedule, BPA, MATOC/MAC, umbrella procurement, and similar vehicles, ask:

```text
How many awardees share the ceiling?
Is the ceiling vehicle-wide or company-specific?
What minimum guarantee exists?
What amount is obligated now?
Must future task orders be competed?
What scope can actually be assigned to this company?
What historical task-order share has the company captured?
What customer funding is visible?
```

Default interpretation:

```text
shared ceiling + little obligated + future competition
→ CE0 / CE1, not CE2+
```

## OSE interaction

Contract Economic state is an input to OSE, not a separate allocator.

Guidance:

- CE0/CE1 may increase Potential, strategic positioning, breadcrumbs, and future-option value.
- CE2 can increase Conversion Confirmation when the commitment is real and material.
- CE3/CE4 strengthen conversion evidence.
- CE5 strengthens profitability transmission and shareholder-capture confidence.
- A very large CE1 opportunity may still deserve little capital if conversion probability, timing, capture, or valuation is weak.
- A smaller CE2/CE3 event may be more economically important than a much larger CE1 headline.

## TTC interaction

For catalyst profitability, use the economic rung that is actually supported.

Do not use:
- vehicle ceiling as event revenue;
- total program size as company backlog;
- optional maximum as committed economics;
- customer budget as company award;
- possible follow-on as current contract value.

The next chronological event and the next economic-conversion event may differ.

Example:

```text
vehicle award (CE1)
→ competed task order (CE2)
→ backlog / delivery (CE3)
→ revenue (CE4)
→ cash / shareholder capture (CE5)
```

TTC should identify the rung transition that is currently decision-relevant.

## Recognition firewall

The CE rung is an **economic-realization state**, not a short-term stock-reaction score.

A CE0/CE1 announcement can still create a large immediate price move because of surprise, narrative intensity, small equity denominator, liquidity, positioning, or recognition dynamics. Conversely, a CE4/CE5 conversion can produce little price movement when it is already expected.

Therefore:

```text
CE STATE / COMMITMENT QUALITY
!=
P(MATERIAL PRICE MOVE | EVENT)
!=
POSITION SIZE
```

Use CE/CQ to constrain claims about committed/realized economics and conversion confidence. Use TTC recognition, repricing burden, remaining opportunity, downside, shareholder capture, and price-response evidence separately to determine capital priority. Quant remains downstream for executable sizing.

A universal CE/CQ-to-weight table is prohibited unless separately validated and promoted.

Validation note: a preregistered eight-case frozen A/B on 2026-09-20 rejected mechanical CE/CQ sizing. The lower-exposure treatment reduced losses in weak/conditional cases but materially undercaptured recognition-driven winners. See `research/system-tests/contract-portfolio-ab-frozen-holdout-2026-09-20.md`.

## Promotion / downgrade

Promote only when new evidence supports the next rung.

Examples:

```text
CE1 -> CE2
new funded task order

CE2 -> CE3
order enters backlog / production / deployment

CE3 -> CE4
reported revenue confirms conversion

CE4 -> CE5
margin / FCF / shareholder economics confirm capture
```

Downgrade or invalidate when:
- funding disappears;
- vehicle expires;
- task order is lost;
- order is cancelled;
- backlog is removed;
- delivery is materially delayed;
- economics prove non-incremental;
- margins / cash conversion fail;
- dilution or financing destroys shareholder capture.

## Anti-double-counting

The same economics move through the ladder.

Do not count the same dollar separately as:
- ceiling value;
- awarded value;
- backlog;
- revenue;
- cash.

Each later rung increases evidence quality and realization, not the underlying nominal economics unless scope itself increases.

## Gauntlet origin

This rule was promoted through a positive/control pair:

- LUNR showed that a large contract vehicle can create a valuable future opportunity surface without the ceiling itself being committed revenue.
- VSEC showed that an enormous shared IDIQ ceiling with negligible initial obligation can produce much smaller realized task-order economics.

The transferable principle is the separation of access/optionality from committed and realized economics.

## Validation boundary

Canonical:
- rung separation;
- Optioned Economic Surface vs Committed Economic Value;
- anti-double-counting;
- vehicle / task-order / backlog / revenue distinction;
- prospective point-in-time use.

Provisional:
- any numeric CE weights;
- transition probabilities;
- universal conversion rates;
- automatic capital multipliers.

Final rule:

> **Measure what the company can access, what the customer has actually committed, what is converting, and what common shareholders can capture. Never let the headline ceiling answer all four questions.**
