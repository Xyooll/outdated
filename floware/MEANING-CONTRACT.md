# Xyool Meaning Contract

Status: **CANDIDATE / VERIFICATION_PENDING**

Purpose: provide a reusable semantic contract for domains where meaning is itself a material object of reasoning, including symbolic, occult, metaphysical, cultural, interpretive, artistic, narrative, and other non-conventional domains.

Core law:

> **Meaning attribution is not the same thing as factual property, causal mechanism, or empirical validation.**

## 1. Why this exists

Some domains reason primarily about symbols, correspondences, archetypes, ritual or metaphysical systems, myths, aesthetic interpretation, spiritual/occult traditions, narrative meaning, or user-created symbolic systems.

Xyool needs to preserve meaning without silently converting it into physical fact or validated prediction.

## 2. Intrinsic versus extrinsic meaning

### Intrinsic meaning

A meaning claimed to belong to the object/pattern itself under a declared system or framework.

INTRINSIC = claim about where the meaning resides; it is not proof that the meaning objectively exists.

An intrinsic meaning must still preserve who or what assigns it.

### Extrinsic meaning

A meaning produced by context, relationship, position, role, observer, history, use, environment, or interaction.

### Composite meaning

Intrinsic meaning + context / relation / role -> composite meaning.

The synthesis must remain reversible enough to recover contributing meanings.

## 3. Meaning object

Required fields when applicable:

MEANING ID; OBJECT / PATTERN ID; MEANING TYPE: INTRINSIC / EXTRINSIC / COMPOSITE / UNKNOWN; CLAIM MODE; SYSTEM / TRADITION / FRAMEWORK; SOURCE / AUTHORSHIP; VERSION; NATIVE MEANING; CONTEXT / RELATION CONDITIONS; SEMANTIC PRIMITIVES; POLARITY / DIRECTION IF USED; DEPENDENCIES; CONFLICTS; BOUNDARIES; EVIDENCE / VALIDATION STATUS; CREATIVE-USE STATUS; SUPERSEDES.

## 4. Claim modes

Possible modes: SYMBOLIC, DESCRIPTIVE, INTERPRETIVE, METAPHYSICAL, RITUAL, NARRATIVE, CREATIVE, CAUSAL, PREDICTIVE, ACTION-GUIDING.

A meaning may be validly represented in one claim mode without earning another.

SOURCE-DOCUMENTED SYMBOLIC MEANING != EMPIRICALLY VALIDATED PREDICTIVE RULE.

## 5. Authorship / provenance

Track separately: TRADITION / SOURCE-DOCUMENTED; USER-DERIVED; XYOOL-DERIVED; OBSERVED-PATTERN CANDIDATE; CREATIVE INVENTION; UNKNOWN.

Do not present a Xyool or user-created meaning as inherited tradition.

## 6. Semantic primitives

When useful, distill elaborate meanings into sparse primitives such as emergence, boundary, exchange, union, loss, transformation, expansion, constraint, recurrence, hiddenness, revelation, creation, destruction, connection, separation.

These primitives support cross-domain correspondence, contradiction detection, corpus comparison, creative recombination, and validation. The original native meaning remains recoverable.

## 7. Intrinsic/extrinsic dependency

Intrinsic and extrinsic meanings may share dependencies. Do not count matching labels as independent confirmations when they derive from the same interpretive lineage.

## 8. Unknown / unresolved meaning

Meaning may remain UNKNOWN, CONTESTED, MULTIPLE VALID SOURCE INTERPRETATIONS, or DEPENDENT ON FRAMEWORK. Do not force consensus.

## 9. Domain establishment

A meaning-dependent domain must establish what objects can carry meaning, intrinsic/extrinsic distinction if applicable, accepted source/provenance classes, composition rules, conflict rules, claim-mode boundaries, and validation requirements appropriate to the claim.

## 10. Creative use

Meaning objects may be used creatively even when empirically unvalidated.

CREATIVE UTILITY != TRUTH AUTHORITY.

A symbolic system can be valuable for art/story/design without promotion to causal or predictive fact.
