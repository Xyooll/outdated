Bind the numerical-magic symbolic language to Floware while preserving the strict separation among exact arithmetic, carrier mappings, symbolic conventions, interpretive hypotheses, and empirical outcome claims.

- carrier and symbolic meanings are domain conventions/rules;
- interpretation is symbolic/research authority;
- empirical efficacy requires frozen target-domain validation;
- no external-action or high-stakes authority is created.

## Domain-native constraints

- grammar/version is explicit;
- arithmetic path is reproducible;
- full reduction chain is preserved;
- AB and BA may share sums while remaining different ordered constructions;
- inherited conventions and local glyph extensions remain distinct;
- real-world outcome claims are never inferred from internal symbolic consistency alone.

## Specialized deterministic support

Existing engine/parser software may support exact:
- sums;
- reduction chains;
- root-family generation;
- letter-number carrier lookup;
- parse verification.

It does not own symbolic interpretation or empirical claims.

## Local calibrations

Remain domain-local:
- 1–9 symbolic definitions;
- zero modifier semantics;
- carrier profiles;
- local glyph extensions;
- repetition/reversal semantics;
- compound-reading style;
- intention-to-root vocabulary.

## Unsupported / blocked capabilities

- empirical efficacy beyond validated scope;
- causal claims from symbolic consistency alone;
- automatic action authority;
- silent grammar/mapping changes.
