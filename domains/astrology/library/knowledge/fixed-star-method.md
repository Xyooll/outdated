## Astronomical method

Every fixed-star contact preserves catalogue identity, epoch, longitude, latitude, optional RA/Dec, magnitude, proper motion when material, target coordinate, and contact method.

Test two contact families separately:
- ecliptic-longitude proximity for common zodiacal fixed-star practice;
- true spherical angular proximity in a common frame/epoch.

## Ptolemaic semantic seed

Ptolemy Tetrabiblos I.9 supplies a historical-primary lineage of planetary analogies. Initial seed candidates:
- Aldebaran: Mars analogue;
- Regulus: Mars + Jupiter;
- Spica: Venus with lesser Mars in the referenced translation;
- Antares: Mars with some Jupiter;
- Fomalhaut: Venus + Mercury;
- Sirius: Jupiter with lesser Mars;
- Procyon: Mercury with lesser Mars;
- Arcturus: Jupiter + Mars.

These are semantic primitives, not event predictions.

## Orb boundary

Historical semantics and Xyool contact thresholds are separate rules. A 1° contact threshold is an XYOOL PILOT HYPOTHESIS. Sensitivity testing should freeze alternatives such as 0.25°, 0.5°, 1°, and 2° before confirmation.

Never widen an orb after reviewing a miss.

## Parans / angularity

Parans and rising/culminating/setting relations require location, sufficiently precise time, explicit calculation method, and fail closed when time precision is inadequate.
