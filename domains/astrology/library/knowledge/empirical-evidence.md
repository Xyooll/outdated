# ASTRO-LIB-EMPIRICAL-EVIDENCE

TYPE: EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE / CRITICAL LITERATURE
STATUS: **SOURCE-GROUNDED**
SCOPE: descriptive/predictive Astrology efficacy claims
LAST REVIEWED: 2026-09-25

Historical or practitioner authority establishes what a system teaches. It does not establish that the system discriminates or predicts.

Controlled studies captured by the Deep Research include:
- Carlson's Nature double-blind natal astrology test;
- Fourie et al. complete-chart/personality work;
- Wyman & Vyse double-blind astrology/personality experiment and Barnum-effect concerns.

These studies do not test Xyool Star Astrology directly. They do establish why subjective 'this fits' scoring is inadequate and why blinding, decoys, inter-rater reliability, controls, and prospective testing matter.

## Domain consequence

Traditional/source-grounded rules remain SOURCE-DOCUMENTED / EMPIRICALLY UNVALIDATED unless appropriate controlled evidence earns a stronger status.

Never equate ancient, official, precisely calculated, or source-documented with predictive validity.
