## Summary

Astrology validation separates:
- input integrity;
- calculation accuracy;
- rule reproducibility;
- descriptive discrimination;
- timing/forecast performance;
- capability translation;
- cross-system convergence;
- cross-case generalization.

Prediction requires:
- pre-outcome freeze;
- finite window;
- hit/miss definitions;
- full miss retention;
- appropriate baselines/controls;
- discovery/confirmation separation;
- calendar-coverage accounting;
- no post-hoc rule/window expansion.

## Boundary

A source-documented rule is not a validated predictive rule.

A coherent story is not predictive evidence.

A forecast hit is not added signal until compared with the declared control/baseline.
