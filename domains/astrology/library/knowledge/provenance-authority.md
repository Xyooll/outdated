TYPE: DOMAIN METHOD

## Orthogonal authority dimensions

Do not force all authority into one score.

Track separately:
- astronomical/calculation authority;
- historical/tradition source authority;
- modern practitioner source authority;
- user-derived / Xyool hypothesis authorship;
- empirical support for the claimed scope.

Example:

Ptolemy may be high-confidence historical evidence for a fixed-star doctrine while that doctrine remains empirically unvalidated.

JPL may be high-authority for an asteroid's position while supplying no astrological meaning.

## Minimum provenance

source_id; author/institution; work/dataset; edition/release; publication date; retrieval date; claim location where practical; language/translator; canonical pointer; version/hash when local; known conflicts; empirical status.

## Failure rule

Never allow source prestige to silently upgrade efficacy status.
