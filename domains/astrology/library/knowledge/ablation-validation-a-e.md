## Model ladder

A — ordinary full chart: major planets/luminaries, angles, houses, signs, rulers/dispositors, major aspects, selected established timing method.

B — A + genuinely additional intrinsic-degree feature such as a frozen 360-degree corpus or degree-node recurrence rule.

C — A + frozen fixed-star neighborhood/contact method.

D — A + B + C.

E — D + preregistered Tier-0 minor-body cohort.

Retaining exact longitude for aspects is not by itself an information ablation if Model A already uses exact longitude.

## Primary question

Score incremental value A->B, A->C, A->D, D->E rather than narrative richness.

## Nulls

Use as applicable: base rate, persistence, calendar/context baseline, matched non-activation windows, shuffled outcomes, shifted timing, rotated-star catalogue, permuted degree-symbol labels, and asteroid semantic-label shuffle.

## Scoring

When calibrated probabilities are permitted, use Brier score and logarithmic score plus calibration, prevalence, false positives/negatives, window coverage, and lead time.

## Discovery / confirmation

Historical cases may discover rules. The same cases cannot confirm them. Preserve a frozen rule cohort and untouched chronological/prospective validation.

Promote richer models only when incremental gain survives frozen rules, controls, untouched data, multiplicity control, acceptable calibration, and replication.
