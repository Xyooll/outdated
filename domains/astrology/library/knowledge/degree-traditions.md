## Historical degree systems

Research categories include monomoiria / degree rulership, degree images, and other explicitly named systems.

Each requires its own source, translation/edition, indexing convention, and system ID. Do not promote modern web summaries into ancient doctrine without the underlying source.

## Star Astrology distinction

Intrinsic degree symbolism != fixed-star meaning != sign/decan/bound meaning != house meaning.

These layers may coexist on one exact-degree node but cannot be counted as independent confirmations by default.
