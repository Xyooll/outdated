## Dependency rule

Cross-corpus agreement is stronger only when the corpora are genuinely independent.

Examples:
- Janduz and La Volasfera are dependency-linked;
- Rudhyar and the original Sabian corpus are dependency-linked;
- multiple modern websites repeating Stojanovic are not independent sources.

## Synthesis target

Xyool should eventually create two learned corpora:

~~~text
XYOOL.LOCAL30.INTRINSIC
30 local-degree meanings

XYOOL.ABS360.INTRINSIC
360 unique absolute-degree meanings
~~~

Each meaning object should preserve:
- contributing source corpora;
- dependent-source groups;
- shared semantic primitives;
- contradictions;
- Xyool synthesis;
- discovery cases;
- untouched validation cases;
- empirical status.

Do not call source consensus empirical validation.
