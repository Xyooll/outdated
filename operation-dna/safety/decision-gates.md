# Decision Gates

All actions proposed by the mind pass through deterministic gates.

## Gate sequence

1. Identity — Is the target known and authorized?
2. Evidence — Is there sufficient provenance?
3. Validity — Does the action satisfy schema and strategy rules?
4. Risk — Is exposure within hard limits?
5. Environment — Are current conditions acceptable?
6. Permission — Is this action allowed for the current node and role?
7. Reversibility — Is there a recovery or rollback path?
8. Audit — Will the action and rationale be recorded?

Failure of a mandatory gate means **reject or hold**, never silently bypass.

AI can request an exception; it cannot grant itself one.
