# Intelligence Maps

Maps represent relationships, not just documents.

## Core maps

- `world-map.md` — geographic nodes and jurisdictions
- `company-map.md` — companies and ownership/operating relationships
- `supply-chain-map.md` — suppliers, bottlenecks, dependencies and customers
- `technology-map.md` — technologies, capabilities and dependencies
- `government-map.md` — agencies, programs, procurement and contracts
- `financial-map.md` — assets, capital structures, financing and market exposure
- `relationship-map.md` — cross-domain relationship index

Every material relationship should point to evidence and carry a confidence score.
