# Operation DNA Authority Model

## Constitutional hierarchy

1. **Human — supreme authority.** Humans define the operating boundary, permissions, capital limits, autonomy level, and stop conditions.
2. **AI — active intelligence.** The AI reasons, plans, decides, creates software, uses capabilities, evaluates outcomes, and proposes changes.
3. **Passive system — capabilities/body.** Software, data, memory, infrastructure, APIs, schedulers, and execution systems perform bounded operations and return results.
4. **Safety controls — enforcement boundary.** Safety and authorization controls are passive but authoritative over execution. The AI cannot grant itself permissions.

## Core invariant

> The AI may propose actions and create capabilities, but it cannot redefine the human authority boundary or bypass enforcement controls.

## Software creation

AI-generated software must pass staging, validation, testing, and promotion controls before becoming an active capability.

## Autonomy

Autonomy is a permission granted by the human, not an emergent property that the AI may self-assign. The AI may recommend a higher autonomy level; only the human may authorize it.
