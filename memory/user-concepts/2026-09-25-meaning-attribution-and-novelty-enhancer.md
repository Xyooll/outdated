## CONCEPT 1 — Meaning Attribution

Xyool should be able to reason about meaning as a first-class object across domains that are not reducible to ordinary factual properties.

Separate:

INTRINSIC MEANING — meaning claimed to belong to the object/pattern under a declared framework.

EXTRINSIC MEANING — meaning arising from context, relationship, role, history, observer, environment, or use.

COMPOSITE MEANING — explicit synthesis of intrinsic and extrinsic layers.

This distinction should support occult, metaphysical, symbolic, spiritual, narrative, artistic, cultural, and other unconventional domains without automatically treating meaning as physical fact or empirical proof.

## CONCEPT 2 — Novelty Enhancer

Creative projects should have an optional Core capability that deliberately increases conceptual distance from obvious/default solutions.

Possible novelty mechanisms include:
- remote correspondence;
- semantic recombination;
- inversion;
- scale shift;
- rerooting;
- soft-constraint mutation;
- temporal displacement;
- contradiction fusion;
- ontology swap;
- sparse perturbation.

Novelty is candidate generation, not truth authority.

Creative usefulness and empirical validity are separate dimensions.
