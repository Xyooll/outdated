> "The other thing is to look at all the characters in the story."

> "VWAV ... talked in many different characters, and its price is directly connected to those."

## CONCEPT

A stock story is made of interacting characters, not only company-level facts.

Material characters can include:
- partners;
- customers;
- regulators;
- financiers;
- lenders;
- acquisition targets;
- subsidiaries;
- insiders;
- governments;
- exchanges;
- other decision makers.

Changes in a character can change the company story and therefore the market's expected value of the company.

## ASSISTANT INTERPRETATION

Use a Character Influence Graph:

```text
CHARACTER STATE
→ CAUSAL RELATIONSHIP
→ COMPANY ECONOMICS / CONTROL / TIMING
→ SHAREHOLDER CAPTURE
→ MARKET RECOGNITION
→ PRICE REPRICING
```

The important object is not the existence of the character but the causal transmission from that character to the security.

## MISS RULE

If a material character was present in available evidence but omitted from the story, that is a mapping miss.
