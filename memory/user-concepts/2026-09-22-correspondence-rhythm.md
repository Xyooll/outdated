## CONCEPT

When a stock is waiting on a catalyst, the timing problem can be approached by identifying the process/protocol that produces the catalyst and comparing its current state with completed structurally corresponding processes.

The relevant analogy is not simply a similar stock.

It is a similar **causal process**.

A correspondence beam is the structural connection between the live case and an analog whose timing mechanism is genuinely similar.
