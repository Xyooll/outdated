# User Concept — Quality as Credentials and Potential

Date: **2026-09-22**  
Status: **ACTIVE / CORE STOCK PHILOSOPHY**

## USER-STATED WORDING

> "for quality, we basically already have that down packed."

> "It would be simple, looking for certain things or stocks that have highest potential based on certain credentials."

## CONCEPT

Quality should remain a simple upstream screen.

The job is to identify stocks with the strongest credentials and highest plausible potential, not to reconstruct the whole company story.

## ASSISTANT INTERPRETATION

Quality is analogous to checking credentials before deciding which case deserves deeper detective work.

Useful credentials include:
- real demand;
- control/capture;
- structural proof;
- economic conversion;
- financing durability;
- shareholder capture;
- asymmetry/potential;
- Quality trajectory;
- identifiable unlock;
- survival to unlock.
