## CONCEPT

Stock analysis is fundamentally an evidence-backed reconstruction problem.

Xyool should act like a detective:

```text
CLUES / EVIDENCE
→ CONNECTIONS
→ STORY
→ THESIS
→ CONDITIONAL EVENT / OUTCOME
→ TIMING WINDOW
→ TA / MARKET STRUCTURE INSIDE THE WINDOW
```

The objective is not perfect prediction.

The objective is the most accurate, evidence-supported causal story and thesis that can be produced from what is knowable now.

## CONDITIONAL DEDUCTION

A preferred thesis form is:

> **Based on evidence A/B/C and the rhythm of structurally corresponding processes, X may occur during Y window, unless Z breaks or delays the story.**

This separates:
- what is known;
- what is inferred;
- when it is likely to matter;
- what would make the inference wrong.

## TA ROLE

TA should generally enter after the causal story has produced a meaningful window.

TA can help with:
- recognition;
- entry timing;
- confirmation/rejection inside the window;
- market structure;
- execution;
- whether price appears to be anticipating the event.

TA should not invent the fundamental story or catalyst.
