## What this archive is

It is an **intellectual continuity record**.

It tries to preserve:
- concepts;
- rationale;
- design philosophy;
- terminology;
- goals;
- evolving beliefs about systems and AI;
- why certain architectural choices were made.

## What this archive is not

It is not:
- a psychological diagnosis;
- a complete biography;
- a private-person profile inferred by AI;
- proof that every stored idea remained permanently held.

Ideas can evolve.

The dated record matters because it shows that evolution.

## How to read authorship

Entries distinguish:

**USER-STATED**  
Words or ideas directly expressed by the user.

**ASSISTANT INTERPRETATION**  
An attempt to organize or explain what the idea may imply.

**SYSTEM CONSEQUENCE**  
How the idea changed Xyool.

If there is ever a conflict, direct user wording outranks assistant interpretation.

## Why it exists

The user explicitly wanted a record that could help:
- their future self remember forgotten ideas;
- a loved one understand the rationale and philosophies behind the system;
- future collaborators see the conceptual path rather than only the finished artifacts.

The archive should therefore remain readable, respectful, and faithful to what was actually expressed.
