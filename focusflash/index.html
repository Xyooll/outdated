Generate speech automatically or tap Record my voice to narrate directly in the app. Microphone recording needs permission. No account, API key, payment, or separate editing app required. Fast voice needs a one-time model download of about 63 MB. Natural voice is larger and slower. Both generate video-ready audio on the device. Video words stay at the selected WPM. Narration is compressed or stretched to match the full passage duration at low volume. Extreme speeds distort pitch; word-by-word phoneme alignment is approximate. Video exports record in real time; keep this tab open until finished. Chrome exports WebM, not MP4.
