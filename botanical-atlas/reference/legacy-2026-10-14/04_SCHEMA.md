# Suggested permanent identity schema

- Stable species ID (taxonomy first, and voucher/lot IDs for specimens)
- Common, scientific, historic and original-language aliases
- Known compounds as child entities: InChIKey / PubChem CID / CAS only after verified lookup
- Species–part–preparation–compound measurement records: units, sample source, methods, replicate count, ranges
- Mechanism: molecular target, pathway, effect direction, concentration, evidence species/cell
- Four-domain map B/P/C/A with null for untested, and source for every positive assertion
- Traditions: geographic and cultural attribution; separate observation from chemical mechanism
- Interaction edges: shared chemical, additive, antagonistic, absorption, toxic, hypothetical, direct chemical reaction
- Safety and contraindications with human evidence priority
- Source PMID/DOI/URL, publication date, language, retraction status, update history

Do not declare completeness of every molecule. Label well-characterized priority inventory versus trace and uncharacterized substances.
