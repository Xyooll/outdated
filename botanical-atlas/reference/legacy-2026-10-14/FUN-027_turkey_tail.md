# FUN-027 — Turkey tail (Trametes versicolor)

**Identity / provenance:** Temperate regions worldwide; intensive Japan/China research  
**Part and preparation scope:** fruiting body and cultured mycelial preparations

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **PSK / krestin** — protein-bound polysaccharide preparation, not a single pure compound.
- **PSP** — distinct polysaccharopeptide product developed in China.
- **β-Glucans** — variable fungal polysaccharides.
- **Ergosterol** — fungal sterol.
- **Trametenolic acid** — triterpenoid reported among related fungal metabolites, verify batch/species.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** No established human energy effect.
- **P – physiological:** PSK has been studied as an ADJUNCT alongside cancer therapy in Japan; cannot replace chemotherapy.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Direct cognition effect unproven.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** No demonstrated mood therapy.

## Evidence level and limits

Human evidence applies to specific standardized PSK/PSP products and clinical contexts.

## Global / traditional context

Yun zhi (cloud mushroom) in Chinese context; kawaratake in Japan.

## Combination map / identity links

FUN-027 ↔ FUN-028 via polysaccharides; clinical products are not interchangeable.

## Safety and unknowns

Immunologic effects may matter with autoimmune conditions/immunosuppressants; quality and species checks.
