# BOT-004 — Peppermint (Mentha × piperita)

**Identity / provenance:** Europe; later widespread  
**Part and preparation scope:** leaves, steam-distilled oil; enteric-coated oil researched separately from leaf tea

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Menthol** — activates cold-sensing TRPM8 and relaxes intestinal smooth muscle.
- **Menthone** — major volatile ketone and possible contributor.
- **Menthyl acetate** — volatile ester, minor individual evidence.
- **1,8-Cineole** — aromatic terpene.
- **Menthofuran** — volatile compound monitored for oil quality.
- **Rosmarinic acid** — leaf phenolic mainly in non-oil extracts.
- **Pulegone** — potentially hepatotoxic constituent controlled in quality standards.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** No persuasive energy effect.
- **P – physiological:** Enteric-coated peppermint oil has mixed but modestly favorable short-term IBS evidence.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Cooling sensation is not brain-cooling or memory improvement.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Comfort from relieved gut symptoms may affect mood indirectly.

## Evidence level and limits

Human clinical trials for formulated oil, evidence low/very low quality overall.

## Global / traditional context

Ancient Egyptian, Greek and European digestive traditions.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-004 ↔ BOT-003/BOT-012 share rosmarinic acid, but not necessarily in their oils.

## Safety and unknowns

Can worsen reflux; concentrates can irritate; high pulegone exposure is unsafe.
