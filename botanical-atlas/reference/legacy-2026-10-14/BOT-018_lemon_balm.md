# BOT-018 — Lemon balm (Melissa officinalis)

**Identity / provenance:** Mediterranean/West Asia; European and Middle Eastern herb traditions  
**Part and preparation scope:** leaves; leaf infusions versus concentrated extracts/oils

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Rosmarinic acid** — major phenolic studied for GABA-metabolism and inflammatory effects.
- **Citral (geranial/neral)** — lemon-smelling aldehydes in essential oil.
- **Citronellal** — volatile aldehyde.
- **Ursolic acid** — triterpenoid.
- **Oleanolic acid** — triterpenoid.
- **Luteolin** — flavonoid.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** No established energy boost.
- **P – physiological:** Some digestive and sleep-related symptom trials, formulations differ.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Small studies explore attention and calmness; results mixed.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Small human trials support possible relaxation/anxiety improvements.

## Evidence level and limits

Human evidence limited and preparations often poorly standardized.

## Global / traditional context

Melissa in European monastery herbal traditions and Middle Eastern use.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-018 ↔ BOT-003 shares rosmarinic acid; BOT-018 ↔ BOT-019 sedating effect may add (not tested).

## Safety and unknowns

May cause sleepiness or interact with sedatives/thyroid medication; essential oil not equivalent to tea.
