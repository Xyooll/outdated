# FUN-025 — Lion’s mane (Hericium erinaceus)

**Identity / provenance:** Asia, Europe, North America  
**Part and preparation scope:** fruiting body and mycelium must be separately tracked

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Hericenones (e.g., C, D, E)** — aromatic compounds characteristic of fruiting body, studied for neurotrophic pathways.
- **Erinacine A** — cyathane diterpene primarily studied from mycelium, neurotrophic preclinical work.
- **Erinacine C** — related mycelial diterpene.
- **Erinacine S** — related.
- **β-Glucans** — cell-wall polysaccharides affecting immune signaling experimentally.
- **Ergothioneine** — sulfur-containing dietary antioxidant transported into human cells.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** No reliable clinical ATP/fatigue enhancement.
- **P – physiological:** Immune pathway claims preclinical.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Small cognitive trials are mixed, with null as well as positive results.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Small stress/mood findings exploratory.

## Evidence level and limits

Human studies preliminary, often use different mushroom parts and formulations.

## Global / traditional context

Houtougu in Chinese culinary/medicinal use; yamabushitake in Japan.

## Combination map / identity links

FUN-025 ↔ FUN-032 fungal β-glucan family overlap but structures differ.

## Safety and unknowns

Allergy and GI reactions possible; do not assume pure erinacine content in fruiting-body supplements.
