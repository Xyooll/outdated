# BOT-011 — Black seed / black cumin (Nigella sativa)

**Identity / provenance:** Southwest Asia and Mediterranean; Unani/Islamicate and African traditions  
**Part and preparation scope:** seeds and pressed seed oil

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Thymoquinone** — quinone studied for inflammatory/redox signaling.
- **Thymohydroquinone** — reduced related phenol with preclinical antimicrobial activity.
- **Dithymoquinone** — dimeric related compound.
- **α-Hederin** — triterpene saponin studied in laboratory models.
- **Nigellidine** — alkaloid with preliminary mechanistic literature.
- **p-Cymene** — volatile terpene.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Small trials suggest improvements in some glycemic measures, uncertain durability.
- **P – physiological:** Human cardiometabolic trials exist, not sufficient for treating infection/cancer.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Neuroprotective hypotheses mainly animal/cell data.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Traditional use for vitality; clinical psychological outcomes uncertain.

## Evidence level and limits

Human pooled trials for selected metabolic outcomes; isolated compounds largely preclinical.

## Global / traditional context

Habba sawda/black seed in West Asian, North African and Unani traditions.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-011 ↔ BOT-023 overlapping metabolic pathways only; synergy hypothesis, not evidence.

## Safety and unknowns

Oil/supplements may interact with diabetes/blood-pressure medications; allergic reactions.
