# BOT-013 — Asian ginseng (Panax ginseng)

**Identity / provenance:** Korea/China/Russian Far East; East Asian medicine  
**Part and preparation scope:** root, white versus steamed red ginseng

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Ginsenoside Rb1** — abundant saponin with experimental neuronal and inflammatory effects.
- **Rg1** — distinct saponin studied for cognition signaling in animals.
- **Re** — protopanaxatriol ginsenoside.
- **Rd** — protopanaxadiol-family member.
- **Rg3** — relatively enriched after steaming.
- **Compound K** — gut-microbial metabolite of several ginsenosides, not necessarily a major raw-root constituent.
- **Gintonin** — non-saponin lipid/protein complex studied experimentally.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Fatigue/glucose studies heterogeneous and preparation-specific.
- **P – physiological:** Vascular and immune effects insufficiently established for disease treatment.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Healthy-human cognitive findings inconsistent.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Stimulation/fatigue perceptions vary; no certain mood benefit.

## Evidence level and limits

Extensive East Asian chemical research; clinical cognition proof not settled.

## Global / traditional context

Ren shen in Chinese medicine; insam in Korea; red ginseng processing matters.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-013 ↔ BOT-032 fungal polysaccharides: immune-pathway overlap not proven synergy; gut microbiota changes ginsenoside metabolites.

## Safety and unknowns

Medication interactions possible, including anticoagulants and diabetes drugs; insomnia possible.
