# FUN-029 — Maitake (Grifola frondosa)

**Identity / provenance:** East Asia, Europe, North America  
**Part and preparation scope:** fruiting bodies, mycelium, D-fraction/MD-fraction extracts

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **D-fraction** — protein-bound β-glucan-rich complex, NOT a single small molecule.
- **MD-fraction** — further purified D-fraction.
- **β-(1,6)/(1,3)-glucans** — structural polysaccharide families.
- **Grifolin** — phenolic compound with mainly preclinical findings.
- **Ergosterol** — fungal sterol.
- **Trehalose** — carbohydrate, not a magic active drug.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Glucose effects preliminary and extract-specific.
- **P – physiological:** Immune cell signaling investigated in laboratory and small studies.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** No established cognition benefit.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** No proven mood effect.

## Evidence level and limits

Many structurally diverse polysaccharides, weak clinical proof for most marketed claims.

## Global / traditional context

Maitake in Japanese culinary tradition, associated with dance of joy.

## Combination map / identity links

FUN-029 ↔ FUN-026/FUN-027 immune polysaccharide pathways; independent validation needed.

## Safety and unknowns

Mushroom allergy; potential interactions with diabetes drugs/anticoagulants.
