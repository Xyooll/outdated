# BOT-016 — Gotu kola (Centella asiatica)

**Identity / provenance:** South/Southeast Asia, tropical Africa; Ayurveda and Chinese traditions  
**Part and preparation scope:** aerial herb and topical preparations

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Asiaticoside** — triterpene glycoside involved in experimental collagen synthesis.
- **Madecassoside** — related triterpene glycoside.
- **Asiatic acid** — aglycone studied for anti-inflammatory/neural mechanisms.
- **Madecassic acid** — related aglycone.
- **Quercetin** — secondary flavonoid.
- **Kaempferol** — secondary flavonoid.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** No demonstrated ATP or metabolic boost.
- **P – physiological:** Topical standardized extracts have some wound/scar clinical investigation, unlike generic mental claims.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Memory benefits mostly traditional/preclinical, not established.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Anxiolytic findings preliminary.

## Evidence level and limits

Much strongest data concern skin repair; brain findings are mechanistic/traditional.

## Global / traditional context

Mandukaparni in Ayurvedic texts; ji xue cao in Chinese materia medica.

## Safety and unknowns

Rare liver injury with some preparations reported; ensure species/authenticity.
