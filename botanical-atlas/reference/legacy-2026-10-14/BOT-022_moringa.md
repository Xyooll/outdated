# BOT-022 — Moringa (Moringa oleifera)

**Identity / provenance:** Indian subcontinent; broad African/South Asian food and medicine  
**Part and preparation scope:** leaves here; seeds, roots and bark require separate profiles

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Glucomoringin** — glucosinolate precursor that can yield moringin.
- **Moringin** — isothiocyanate with cell-signaling effects in vitro.
- **Quercetin** — flavonoid.
- **Kaempferol** — flavonoid.
- **Chlorogenic acid** — phenolic linked to glucose-handling research.
- **Niazirin** — glycoside studied preclinically.
- **β-Carotene** — provitamin A nutrient.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Leaves provide nutrients; extract-specific diabetes/metabolic claims not clinically settled.
- **P – physiological:** Nutritional food value is clearer than disease-treatment efficacy.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** No compelling human cognition proof.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** No robust mood trials.

## Evidence level and limits

Global nutrition interest; much metabolic research animal/cell-based.

## Global / traditional context

Sahjan/drumstick tree in South Asia; widespread food and folk use in Africa.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-022 ↔ BOT-011 share lab glycemic pathways; mechanism overlap does not establish safe combination.

## Safety and unknowns

Leaf food use differs from concentrated root/bark products; pregnancy and quality cautions.
