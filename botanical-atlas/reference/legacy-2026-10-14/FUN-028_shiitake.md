# FUN-028 — Shiitake (Lentinula edodes)

**Identity / provenance:** East Asia; Japanese/Chinese culinary traditions  
**Part and preparation scope:** edible fruiting body; purified lentinan used in some clinical research

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Lentinan** — β-(1,3)-glucan with branches, studied for immune modulation.
- **Eritadenine** — unusual purine derivative associated with lipid changes in animal models.
- **Lenthionine** — volatile sulfur aroma compound.
- **Lentinic acid** — precursor in aroma chemistry.
- **Ergothioneine** — sulfur-containing antioxidant nutrient.
- **Ergosterol** — vitamin-D2 precursor under UV.
- **β-Glucans** — broader fiber/polysaccharide category.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Food nutrients aid normal metabolism; no special ATP claim.
- **P – physiological:** Human data on immune outcomes limited; lentinan injection studies differ greatly from cooked shiitake.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** No robust cognition evidence.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Flavor and food experience, not demonstrated mood effect.

## Evidence level and limits

Good culinary nutrition evidence; many isolated-compound health claims preclinical.

## Global / traditional context

Shiitake in Japan, xianggu in China; historically food and medicinal formulas.

## Combination map / identity links

FUN-028 ↔ FUN-027 beta-glucan overlap, not equivalent clinical products.

## Safety and unknowns

Raw/undercooked shiitake can cause shiitake dermatitis; fungal allergies.
