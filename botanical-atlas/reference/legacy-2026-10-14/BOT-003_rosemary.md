# BOT-003 — Rosemary (Salvia rosmarinus (syn. Rosmarinus officinalis))

**Identity / provenance:** Mediterranean; European/Maghrebi traditions  
**Part and preparation scope:** leaves; oil versus polyphenol-rich extracts

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Carnosic acid** — fat-soluble antioxidant-response regulator studied in cells/animals.
- **Carnosol** — oxidized derivative with preclinical inflammatory effects.
- **Rosmarinic acid** — water/alcohol-extractable phenolic.
- **1,8-Cineole** — volatile monoterpene with sensory and airway effects.
- **Camphor** — neuroactive ketone toxic at excessive exposure.
- **α-Pinene** — volatile scent compound.
- **Ursolic acid** — triterpene with preclinical metabolic/anti-inflammatory findings.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Metabolic and mitochondrial effects primarily preclinical.
- **P – physiological:** Topical/sensory uses; human systemic effects not well established.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Small fragrance studies suggest possible attention associations; no reliable memory treatment.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Aroma may influence subjective alertness.

## Evidence level and limits

Chemistry well characterized; most compound mechanisms are nonhuman.

## Global / traditional context

Mediterranean kitchen/ritual fragrance; global medicinal use.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-003 ↔ BOT-004 and BOT-012 (rosmarinic acid); BOT-003 ↔ BOT-016 (ursolic acid in other plants).

## Safety and unknowns

Concentrated essential oil and camphor can cause seizures/poisoning; caution pregnancy and children.
