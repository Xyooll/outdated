# BOT-014 — Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

**Identity / provenance:** India, Middle East, North Africa; Ayurveda  
**Part and preparation scope:** root or leaves; withanolide profile differs across plant parts

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Withanolide A** — steroidal lactone studied in preclinical neural models.
- **Withaferin A** — potent steroidal lactone especially relevant to leaf extracts, cytotoxic at some concentrations.
- **Withanoside IV** — glycosylated withanolide studied in animals.
- **Withanoside V** — related constituent.
- **Sitoindosides** — glycosylated steroid constituents reported in preparations.
- **Somniferine** — alkaloid mentioned in phytochemical literature.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Not demonstrated to raise cellular energy directly.
- **P – physiological:** Possible sleep/stress-related physiological changes; clinical findings product-specific.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Cognition claims less secure than short-term stress/sleep outcomes.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Small randomized trials show possible stress and sleep benefit.

## Evidence level and limits

Human trials exist; individual-molecule mechanisms mainly nonhuman.

## Global / traditional context

Rasayana herb in Ayurveda; this is traditional classification, not proof of rejuvenation.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-014 ↔ BOT-016 mood/stress pathways conceptually overlap; interaction untested.

## Safety and unknowns

Rare liver injury reports, thyroid effects, pregnancy caution; product quality matters.
