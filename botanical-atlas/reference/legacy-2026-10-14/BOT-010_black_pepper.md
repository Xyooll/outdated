# BOT-010 — Black pepper (Piper nigrum)

**Identity / provenance:** South India; global spice and traditional medicines  
**Part and preparation scope:** peppercorn fruits and piperine-enriched extracts

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Piperine** — pungent alkaloid affecting drug transport/metabolism in lab and some human pharmacokinetic research.
- **Piperettine** — related piperamide with sparse human study.
- **Piperanine** — minor piperamide.
- **β-Caryophyllene** — aromatic sesquiterpene shared with clove.
- **Limonene** — minor volatile terpene.
- **Sabinene** — aromatic monoterpene.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Metabolic enhancement claims generally speculative.
- **P – physiological:** Drug-absorption interaction more established than therapeutic effects.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** No proven cognition enhancement.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Pungency/aroma may affect subjective arousal.

## Evidence level and limits

Mechanistic and pharmacokinetic findings strongest; wellness efficacy limited.

## Global / traditional context

Maricha in Ayurveda; central in South Asian trade and medicine.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-010 ↔ BOT-006 alters curcumin exposure; also potentially alters prescription-drug pharmacokinetics.

## Safety and unknowns

Piperine concentrates can raise exposure to some medicines; culinary black pepper is not equivalent to concentrated extract.
