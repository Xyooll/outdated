# FUN-026 — Reishi / lingzhi (Ganoderma lingzhi / Ganoderma lucidum species complex)

**Identity / provenance:** East Asia and cosmopolitan relatives; taxonomy frequently misapplied  
**Part and preparation scope:** fruiting body, spores, mycelium differ; verify DNA and species

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Ganoderic acid A** — lanostane triterpene with preclinical pathways.
- **Ganoderic acid C2** — related triterpene.
- **Ganoderic acid DM** — autophagy/apoptosis studies mainly cells.
- **Lucidenic acids** — triterpenoid family.
- **β-Glucans** — polysaccharides engaging immune recognition experimentally.
- **LZ-8** — fungal immunomodulatory protein.
- **Ergosterol** — fungal sterol.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** No clinically established metabolic benefit; some controlled results null.
- **P – physiological:** Immune-marker studies do not prove enhanced infection defense.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Neuroprotection largely preclinical.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Longevity/relaxation claims mainly traditional.

## Evidence level and limits

Many compounds identified; controlled human evidence modest and not clearly efficacious for blood glucose, pressure or cholesterol.

## Global / traditional context

Lingzhi in Chinese traditions and reishi in Japan; historical longevity symbolism.

## Combination map / identity links

FUN-026 ↔ FUN-027 via immunomodulatory polysaccharides; additivity remains unproved.

## Safety and unknowns

Possible bleeding interactions and liver problems with some extracts; taxonomic mislabeling common.
