# BOT-021 — Frankincense / Indian boswellia (Boswellia serrata)

**Identity / provenance:** Indian subcontinent; Ayurveda  
**Part and preparation scope:** tree resin; not the same as essential oil

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **AKBA (acetyl-11-keto-β-boswellic acid)** — pentacyclic triterpene studied for inflammatory enzymes/pathways.
- **KBA (11-keto-β-boswellic acid)** — related acid.
- **β-Boswellic acid** — resin triterpene.
- **Acetyl-β-boswellic acid** — related ester.
- **Incensole acetate** — neuroactive diterpene reported from some Boswellia species/resins; check species, do not attribute uncritically.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** No established metabolic energy effects.
- **P – physiological:** Small osteoarthritis trials suggest pain/function benefit for specific formulations.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** No established cognition benefit.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Resin smell may change subjective experience; psychiatric effects not established.

## Evidence level and limits

Human joint trials exist, often small/industry-associated; isolated compounds mostly preclinical.

## Global / traditional context

Shallaki in Ayurveda; distinguish Boswellia serrata from other frankincense species.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-021 ↔ BOT-006 shared inflammatory targets, but dose/formulation synergy unproven.

## Safety and unknowns

GI upset and interaction potential; do not equate frankincense aroma oil with boswellic acid extract.
