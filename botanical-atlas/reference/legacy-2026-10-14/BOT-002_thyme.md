# BOT-002 — Thyme (Thymus vulgaris)

**Identity / provenance:** Mediterranean; European/Middle Eastern herbal traditions  
**Part and preparation scope:** leaf and flowering tops; chemotypes differ markedly

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Thymol** — volatile phenol, disrupts microbial membranes in vitro.
- **Carvacrol** — thymol isomer with similar lab antimicrobial activity.
- **p-Cymene** — aromatic terpene, often a co-component.
- **γ-Terpinene** — terpene precursor and aroma component.
- **Rosmarinic acid** — polyphenol with preclinical inflammatory signaling effects.
- **Luteolin** — flavone studied for anti-inflammatory actions.
- **Apigenin** — flavone studied for nervous-system interactions.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Human metabolic effects inadequately measured.
- **P – physiological:** Traditional cough/digestive applications; antimicrobial effects mostly laboratory evidence.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** No confirmed cognition outcome.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Aroma may feel soothing; not clinically established anxiolytic therapy.

## Evidence level and limits

Chemistry strong; clinical results depend on extract and indication.

## Global / traditional context

Mediterranean household herb and European herbal medicines.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-002 ↔ BOT-004 (rosmarinic acid); BOT-002 ↔ BOT-012 (aromatic-phenol overlap, not proven synergy).

## Safety and unknowns

Essential oil can irritate mucosa/skin; do not assume undiluted oil is safe to ingest.
