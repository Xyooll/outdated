# BOT-017 — Saffron (Crocus sativus)

**Identity / provenance:** Iran and Mediterranean/South Asia; Persian traditional medicine  
**Part and preparation scope:** red stigmas; extract standardization crucial

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Crocin** — water-soluble carotenoid glycoside with preclinical neural/redox effects.
- **Crocetin** — metabolite/aglycone of crocins.
- **Safranal** — aroma volatile studied for nervous-system effects.
- **Picrocrocin** — bitter glycoside precursor of safranal.
- **Kaempferol derivatives** — secondary flavonoids.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Metabolic evidence preliminary.
- **P – physiological:** Potential impacts on pain/other outcomes under study.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Cognition findings early and inconsistent.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Multiple small RCTs and meta-analyses suggest depressive symptom benefits; bias/publication concerns.

## Evidence level and limits

Human mood trials exist for saffron extracts, not proof for each single molecule.

## Global / traditional context

Zafaran in Persian/Unani traditions; Iranian studies important primary evidence.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-017 ↔ BOT-014 both researched for stress/mood, but interaction has not been established.

## Safety and unknowns

High amounts can be toxic; pregnancy and medication cautions; counterfeiting common.
