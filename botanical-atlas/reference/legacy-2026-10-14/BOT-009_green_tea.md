# BOT-009 — Green tea (Camellia sinensis)

**Identity / provenance:** China origin; East Asian and worldwide tea cultures  
**Part and preparation scope:** minimally oxidized leaves; brewed tea versus high-dose extracts

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **EGCG** — major catechin; affects oxidative/inflammatory pathways in vitro.
- **EGC** — related catechin.
- **ECG** — gallated catechin.
- **EC** — catechin.
- **L-Theanine** — amino acid associated with attentional and relaxation effects in some human studies.
- **Caffeine** — adenosine receptor antagonist, reliably increases wakefulness.
- **Quercetin** — minor flavonoid depending on product.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Caffeine acutely increases perceived energy, not cellular ATP production; other metabolic effects generally modest.
- **P – physiological:** Some lipid/vascular effects; concentrated catechin exposure can harm liver.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Caffeine raises alertness; theanine/caffeine combinations studied for attention.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Caffeine can worsen anxiety; theanine may soften subjective tension in some contexts.

## Evidence level and limits

Strong caffeine evidence, moderate/variable human trials for tea mixtures, mechanistic for catechins.

## Global / traditional context

Tea cultures in China, Japan, Korea and beyond.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-009 ↔ BOT-024 coffee shares caffeine; caffeine + theanine are an investigated functional combination within tea.

## Safety and unknowns

Extracts are riskier than customary brewed tea; sleep, drug and iron-absorption cautions.
