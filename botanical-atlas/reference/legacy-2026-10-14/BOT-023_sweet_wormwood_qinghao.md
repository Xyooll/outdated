# BOT-023 — Sweet wormwood / qinghao (Artemisia annua)

**Identity / provenance:** China; now grown globally; Chinese materia medica  
**Part and preparation scope:** aerial parts; artemisinin quantity varies greatly with growth/process

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Artemisinin** — sesquiterpene endoperoxide that generates reactive intermediates in malaria parasites.
- **Artemisinic acid** — biosynthetic precursor, not equal to artemisinin activity.
- **Arteannuin B** — related sesquiterpene.
- **Casticin** — methoxylated flavonoid.
- **Chrysosplenol D** — flavonoid studied for potential interactions.
- **Chrysosplenetin** — related flavonoid.
- **Scopoletin** — coumarin.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Not established as metabolic enhancer.
- **P – physiological:** Purified artemisinin-derived DRUG combinations are important antimalarials; herbal tea is NOT a reliable malaria treatment.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Neurological benefits unproven.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** No established mood treatment.

## Evidence level and limits

High-quality drug evidence cannot be transferred to uncontrolled plant preparation.

## Global / traditional context

Qinghao recorded in Chinese medicine; modern antimalarial drug discovery arose from revisiting this tradition.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-023 ↔ BOT-010: drug-metabolism interaction concerns, not a DIY combination.

## Safety and unknowns

Do not use herbal preparations for malaria in place of approved ACT; resistance and variable dosing are major dangers.
