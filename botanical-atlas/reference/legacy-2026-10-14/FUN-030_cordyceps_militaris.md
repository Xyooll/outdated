# FUN-030 — Cordyceps militaris (Cordyceps militaris)

**Identity / provenance:** Asia and global cultivation; historical East Asian cordyceps traditions  
**Part and preparation scope:** fruiting body, mycelium and broth; not interchangeable with Ophiocordyceps sinensis

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Cordycepin (3′-deoxyadenosine)** — nucleoside analog studied for RNA/metabolic signaling, quickly metabolized.
- **Adenosine** — nucleoside with ubiquitous physiological signaling.
- **Cordycepic acid (mannitol)** — common sugar alcohol, not uniquely active.
- **β-Glucans** — polysaccharides investigated for immune effects.
- **Ergosterol** — sterol.
- **Militarinone-family metabolites** — reported fungal secondary compounds, context and species verification needed.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Exercise/ATP enhancement not robustly proven in humans.
- **P – physiological:** Immunomodulatory and antitumor claims largely preclinical.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** No reliable cognition benefit.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Vitality symbolism; clinical mood evidence lacking.

## Evidence level and limits

Human trials small, products variable; isolated cordycepin mostly cell/animal evidence.

## Global / traditional context

Related to dong chong xia cao traditions but biologically distinct from wild Ophiocordyceps sinensis.

## Combination map / identity links

FUN-030 ↔ BOT-013 both marketed for fatigue; actual combination trials needed.

## Safety and unknowns

Allergy, product contamination/mislabelling, potential medication interactions.
