# BOT-006 — Turmeric (Curcuma longa)

**Identity / provenance:** South Asia; Ayurveda and regional food traditions  
**Part and preparation scope:** rhizome; curcuminoid extracts differ from turmerone-rich oil

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Curcumin** — polyphenol studied for inflammatory signaling, but has low oral bioavailability.
- **Demethoxycurcumin** — related curcuminoid.
- **Bisdemethoxycurcumin** — related curcuminoid.
- **ar-Turmerone** — aromatic essential-oil sesquiterpene studied preclinically.
- **α-Turmerone** — volatile compound with preliminary biological studies.
- **β-Turmerone** — oil constituent with limited human evidence.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Mechanistic metabolic benefits uncertain in people.
- **P – physiological:** Some supplement trials suggest improvements in selected pain/inflammatory outcomes; results depend on formula.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Neuroprotective lab hypotheses not equivalent to cognition gains.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Mood findings preliminary and formulation-specific.

## Evidence level and limits

Human trials exist for particular curcumin products, not every turmeric extract.

## Global / traditional context

Haldi in Ayurveda, South Asian foods and rituals.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-006 ↔ BOT-010 piperine alters absorption and potentially medication exposure; caution rather than automatic synergy.

## Safety and unknowns

Some concentrated high-bioavailability curcumin products linked to liver injury; pregnancy/medication cautions.
