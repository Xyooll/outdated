# BOT-024 — Coffee (Coffea arabica)

**Identity / provenance:** East Africa origin; worldwide drinking cultures  
**Part and preparation scope:** roasted beans, brewed drink; filtered/unfiltered differences

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Caffeine** — blocks adenosine receptors, increases wakefulness.
- **Chlorogenic acids** — polyphenols linked to glucose metabolism and microbial metabolites.
- **Trigonelline** — nitrogenous alkaloid that changes with roasting.
- **Cafestol** — diterpene retained in unfiltered coffee and can raise LDL.
- **Kahweol** — related diterpene.
- **Melanoidins** — complex roasting reaction products, not a single molecule.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Perceived energy/alertness reliably increases acutely via caffeine.
- **P – physiological:** Unfiltered brewing can raise LDL via diterpenes; observational health correlations are not proof of causation.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Acute attention/vigilance benefit from caffeine.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Can worsen anxiety, jitters and sleep, especially at higher intake.

## Evidence level and limits

Strong human stimulant evidence; other health outcomes mixed observational/trial evidence.

## Global / traditional context

African origin, Arabian coffee culture and worldwide traditions.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-024 ↔ BOT-009 shared caffeine; two drinks add caffeine exposures.

## Safety and unknowns

Sleep disruption, palpitations and anxiety; pregnancy and medication cautions.
