# BOT-015 — Bacopa / brahmi (Bacopa monnieri)

**Identity / provenance:** South Asian wetlands; Ayurveda  
**Part and preparation scope:** whole aerial herb, standardized bacoside extracts

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **Bacoside A** — not a single molecule but a mixture of saponins, linked to preclinical synaptic effects.
- **Bacoside A3** — individual major saponin.
- **Bacopaside II** — related saponin.
- **Bacopasaponin C** — triterpenoid saponin.
- **Betulinic acid** — triterpene with preclinical effects.
- **Loliolide** — small lactone.
- **Quercetin** — flavonoid.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Mitochondrial protection is experimental, not validated human energy enhancement.
- **P – physiological:** GI effects common in trials.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** Some trials suggest small delayed memory/attention benefits with standardized extracts over weeks.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** Anxiety/stress findings less consistent.

## Evidence level and limits

Human cognitive trials limited and product-dependent; compound mechanisms mainly preclinical.

## Global / traditional context

Brahmi name is also used for gotu kola in some regions—avoid identity mix-ups.

## Combination map / identity links

BOT-015 ↔ BOT-016 share memory-related traditional labels, but chemically distinct.

## Safety and unknowns

Nausea/diarrhea and possible drug interactions; pregnancy safety unestablished.
