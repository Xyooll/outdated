# FUN-031 — Oyster mushroom (Pleurotus ostreatus)

**Identity / provenance:** Cosmopolitan; global culinary cultivation  
**Part and preparation scope:** cooked fruiting body

## Known / reported priority compounds

- **β-Glucans** — dietary fibers and fungal immune-recognition ligands.
- **Ergothioneine** — antioxidant nutrient via dedicated human transporter.
- **Lovastatin** — reported in certain Pleurotus samples, variable and usually not a reliable drug dose.
- **Ergosterol** — UV-sensitive precursor of vitamin D2.
- **Pleuran** — named oyster mushroom β-glucan preparation.
- **Phenolic acids** — low/variable bioactive class.

## Four-domain effects (not all clinically proven)

- **B – bioenergetic/metabolic:** Nutritious food; special metabolic efficacy not reliably shown.
- **P – physiological:** Fiber effects plausible; immune/LDL claims cannot be inferred from any mushroom serving.
- **C – cognitive/neurocognitive:** No conclusive cognitive enhancement.
- **A – affective/psychophysiological:** No proven mood therapy.

## Evidence level and limits

Human nutrition evidence stronger than mushroom-derived drug claims.

## Global / traditional context

Widespread edible mushroom, including Asian/European cooking.

## Combination map / identity links

FUN-031 ↔ FUN-028 ergothioneine and beta glucan overlap, quantities variable.

## Safety and unknowns

Food allergy possible; always cook; substrate affects metal/contaminant content.
